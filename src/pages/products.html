GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you
make room.

Do I need council approval for a tiny home or pod?+

Requirements depend on your local council, site, intended use and the selected model. Please check with your local authority and qualified professionals before purchase. Our team can help discuss the product information relevant to your plans.

How long does delivery take?+

Delivery timing varies by model, stock status, configuration and destination. Contact our team for current availability and an estimate for your location.

Can I customise the layout or finishes?+

Options depend on the model. Ask us about available layouts, finishes and configurations for the space you are considering.

Are off-grid options available?+

Features and utility connections vary by product. Tell us about your site and intended use so we can discuss what is available for your chosen model.

What warranty comes with a product?+

Warranty information varies by product. Please request the current warranty terms for your model before placing an order.

Can I get finance for a micro home?+

Finance options may be available, subject to provider terms, eligibility, assessment and approval. Visit our finance page for indicative information.
